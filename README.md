## 📂 Projects

### 1. Online Store Analysis
**Dataset:** 1200 e-commerce orders  
**Focus:** Order status breakdown, product performance, revenue analysis  

**Key Findings:**
- 20.83% cancellation rate (more cancelled than delivered)
- Chair generates highest revenue through bulk buying (3.16 avg qty/order)
- 41.4% of orders fail (cancelled + returned)

**Skills:** SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, aggregations, business metrics
